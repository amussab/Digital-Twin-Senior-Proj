## Limitations (state alongside the number)

1. **Circularity.** The twin's stiffness trajectory K(t) = K0 (1 - 0.25 damage) is derived from the same synthetic damage that drives the accelerometer features and the RUL label; agreement here is agreement of two estimators of one latent variable on generated data, not evidence about real bearings.
2. **The physics value is mostly the prior.** In stages 1-3 K drops by <= ~5 % against ~6 % (1750 rpm) / ~25 % (3600 rpm) per-window identification noise, so the twin's RUL is the rated-life prior max(L_ref - age, 0.1 L_ref) on 97.4 % of windows. IS2 v2 therefore checks that the AI stays within the physically plausible envelope of the twin (no gross bias, no inverted trend), not that the twin independently confirms the AI's accuracy; accuracy is S7 / IS3.
3. **3600 rpm identification gate.** K is weakly observable above the bearing mode; the gate rejects most 3600 rpm windows, so at 3600 rpm the physics RUL is the prior almost everywhere.
4. **L_ref is synthetic.** L_ref = median TRAIN-run life per speed (72.9 h at 1750, 46.5 h at 3600 rpm); on the rig it becomes the ISO 281 L10 (or L50) at the duty point, and the thresholds must be re-derived on rig validation data.
5. **v3c is research.** It consumes RUL_physics as an input, so its residual measures consistency, not independent validation.
6. Thresholds were set from the deployed v3b on validation; the median clause separates v3b from an uninformative constant AI by a small margin (see controls).
