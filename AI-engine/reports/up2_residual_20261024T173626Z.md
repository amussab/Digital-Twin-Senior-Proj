### Limitations to state alongside the number

1. **Circularity.** The stiffness trajectory K(t) fed to the FE twin is derived from the same synthetic damage curve that drives the generator's features and RUL label.  Agreement here is agreement of two estimators of one latent variable, not evidence about real bearings.
2. **Floor set by the AI.** The AI RUL has its own error vs truth, so even a perfect physics RUL gives a residual of 16.7 % median / 175.7 % max (row 'perfect physics' below).  A <= 5 % IS2 would need the AI to be within ~5 % of truth, which S7 (MAPE 15 %) does not require.
3. **Stage 1 has no trend.** Before onset K is constant; the trend-based physics RUL is (correctly) silent there.  Coverage by stage is in the JSON.
4. **3600 rpm.** K is weakly observable above the bearing mode (twin README); the identification gate drops most such windows, so 3600 rpm runs contribute little or no physics RUL (coverage by rpm below).
5. Identification and FE model are the twin's initial, replaceable version with ASSUMED geometry (`twin/params.py`).
