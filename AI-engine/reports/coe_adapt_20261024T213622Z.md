## Caveats

- [SIMULATION] the simulator's transmission (off-plane gain 0.25-0.45 of the partner's impacts), resonance, noise and amplitudes are invented; the real rig's transmission path and noise floor are unknown until hardware data exists. Re-fit the calibration / gate threshold on commissioning + seeded-fault data from the rig.
- A per-bearing classifier cannot separate 'small own defect' from 'partner defect transmitted' from its own 16 features; the gate uses the partner bearing of the same window for that.
- RUL/stage heads were not re-fitted: COE simulator runs are not run-to-failure (no truth for RUL). The diagnostic above shows the unchanged v3b stage head is NOT usable on COE-pipeline features: healthy COE bearings get raw z around -0.1 (synth_v3 healthy target -0.2, stage cut -0.25), i.e. stage >= 2 on every healthy window. Fix = COE run-to-failure trajectories (simulator severity driven by the synth_v3 damage curve) and a COE-refitted z/RUL head, or a z_cut recalibrated on commissioning data; not done here.
- Missed faults rise with the gate (22.8 % on test): mostly the lowest severities (ramp starts, severity <= 0.2), where the defect is weaker than the threshold and, in both-faulted runs, the weaker of two same-family defects is suppressed by design.
